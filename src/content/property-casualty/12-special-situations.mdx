# 12. Special situations and pointers

- **Citizens Property Insurance Corporation.** Citizens is the state-created
  insurer of last resort and carries its own eligibility rules and, in some
  respects, distinct procedures. A Citizens policyholder should review
  Citizens-specific requirements in addition to the general framework above.
- **Flood damage is different.** Standard homeowner policies exclude flood.
  Flood coverage almost always comes from the federal **National Flood
  Insurance Program (NFIP)** (or a private flood policy), which has its **own
  claim and appeal procedures and deadlines** that are **not** governed by the
  Florida statutes in this guide.
- **Surplus lines carriers.** Some Florida property is insured by eligible
  surplus lines insurers. Many of the statutes above expressly reach surplus
  lines residential coverage, but a few provisions treat surplus lines
  differently, so the specific carrier type can matter.
- **Hurricanes and states of emergency.** After a declared emergency, the
  Office of Insurance Regulation may extend certain insurer deadlines, and the
  volume of claims can lengthen real-world timelines even where statutory
  deadlines are unchanged.
- **Roof-specific rules.** Florida has specific provisions governing roof
  deductibles and how much of a roof loss an insurer may pay at ACV pending
  proof of repair; these can affect the amount of an early payment on a roof
  claim.
