# Glossary

- **ACV (Actual Cash Value):** Replacement cost minus depreciation.
- **ADR:** Alternative Dispute Resolution — mediation, appraisal, or
  arbitration used instead of a trial.
- **Adverse adjudication:** A court determination and final judgment against
  the insurer; the prerequisite for a property bad-faith suit under
  § 624.1551.
- **Appraisal:** A policy-based process that decides the **amount of loss**
  (not coverage), using each side's appraiser and a neutral umpire.
- **Bad faith:** An insurer's failure to handle a claim honestly and fairly;
  the basis for a separate statutory action under § 624.155.
- **Condition precedent:** A step that must be completed before a right (such
  as the right to sue) can be exercised.
- **CRN (Civil Remedy Notice):** The DFS-filed notice required before a
  statutory bad-faith suit; triggers a 60-day cure period.
- **Date of loss:** The date fixed by statute or the date the damaging event
  occurred; the point from which the two main deadlines run.
- **EUO (Examination Under Oath):** A recorded, sworn questioning of the
  policyholder that many policies allow the insurer to require.
- **First-party claim:** A claim under one's own policy against one's own
  insurer.
- **NOI / Notice of Intent:** The pre-suit Notice of Intent to Initiate
  Litigation required by § 627.70152.
- **Post-loss obligations:** Policy conditions the insured must satisfy after
  a loss (notice, mitigation, documentation, cooperation, EUO, proof of loss).
- **Proof of loss:** A (often sworn) statement itemizing the claimed loss and
  its value.
- **RCV (Replacement Cost Value):** The cost to repair or replace without a
  depreciation deduction.
- **Reservation of rights:** An insurer's notice that it is investigating but
  reserving the right to deny; not itself a denial.
- **Umpire:** The neutral third person in an appraisal who breaks a tie
  between the two appraisers.
