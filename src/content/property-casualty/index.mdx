# Florida Property Insurance Disputes

**A guide for policyholders handling a first-party residential property insurance claim.**

<Disclaimer />

---

## What this guide covers

First-party residential property insurance claims in Florida — disputes a
homeowner, condominium unit owner, or renter brings under their own policy
against their own insurer for physical damage to the insured property.
Typical losses: hurricane and windstorm damage, water damage from a burst
pipe or roof leak, fire, and theft.

## The two clocks

The single most important thing to understand: **two entirely separate
deadlines run at the same time**, measured from the date of loss.

| The clock | Statute | Length | Runs from | What it controls |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Report the claim** | § 627.70132 | **1 year** | date of loss | Deadline to notify the insurer of a new or reopened claim |
| **Report a supplemental claim** | § 627.70132 | **18 months** | date of loss | Deadline to notify the insurer of additional damage |
| **File a lawsuit** | § 95.11(2)(e) | **5 years** | date of loss | Statute of limitations to sue for breach of contract |

> **The report-clock and sue-clock are independent.** Reporting on time does
> not satisfy the filing deadline. A timely suit fails if the claim was never
> reported within the reporting window.
